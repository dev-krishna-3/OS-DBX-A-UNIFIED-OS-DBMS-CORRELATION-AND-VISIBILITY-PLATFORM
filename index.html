Words to know

Each one in plain language, with the shop idea alongside.

CPU
The brain of the computer. It can only work on one job at a time.
In the shop: the cashier.
Process
A program that is running, or waiting to run.
In the shop: a customer.
Arrival time AT
The moment a process joins the waiting line.
In the shop: when the customer walks in.
Burst time BT
How much CPU time a process needs to finish its job.
In the shop: how long they need at the till.
Waiting time
How long a process sits in line without being served.
In the shop: time spent queueing.
Turnaround time
From joining the line to being completely done. It equals waiting plus running.
In the shop: walking in to walking out.
Priority
How important a job is. In this lab, 1 is the most important.
In the shop: a customer with a fast pass.
Time quantum
The longest turn a process can have in Round robin.
In the shop: a timer on each customer.

Think of a shop with one cashier

  • The CPU is the cashier.

    It can only serve one customer at a time.

  • Each process is a customer.

    A program waiting for its turn.

  • A rule decides who is next.

    First come first served, shortest first, most important first, or taking turns.

  • Your job: try, watch, compare.

    Change the rule, run it, and see who waits the longest.
